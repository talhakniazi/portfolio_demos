How we started

{{ business.short_name }} began in {{ business.founded }} with two cleaners and a van, cleaning homes in south Manchester. Most of our early customers came through recommendations, and they still do.

Today we clean homes, rental properties and offices across {{ business.area }}. We've stayed deliberately local, so a supervisor is never far from any job.

Every cleaner is employed by us, trained in-house and works to written checklists for each type of clean. We use the professional colour-coded cloth system in every property, and we bring our own eco-friendly products and equipment.
