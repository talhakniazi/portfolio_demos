What a regular clean covers, room by room

Each room gets its own colour of cloth, so nothing is carried from the bathroom to the kitchen.

Kitchen

  • Worktops and splashbacks wiped and sanitised
  • Hob cleaned, outside of appliances wiped
  • Sink and taps descaled and polished
  • Floor hoovered and mopped

Bathrooms

  • Bath, shower and tiles cleaned
  • Basins and taps descaled
  • Toilets cleaned inside and out (red cloths only)
  • Mirrors polished, floor mopped

Living areas and bedrooms

  • Dusting, including high and low surfaces
  • Furniture and glass polished
  • Carpets and rugs hoovered
  • Beds made and bins emptied
